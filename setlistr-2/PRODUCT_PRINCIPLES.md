# Setlistr Product Principles

> The emotional brief that governs every product decision.

## The goal

Make artists feel that what they do on stage matters, is remembered, and is building toward something real.

## One emotional focus per screen

| Screen | The artist should feel |
|--------|----------------------|
| Home | "My career is growing." |
| Live Capture | "Setlistr is listening." |
| Post-Show | "Tonight mattered." |
| Submission | "Get what you earned." |
| Onboarding | "This is the beginning of something real." |
| Stats | "Look how far I've come." |

## The ten principles

1. **One emotional focus per screen.** If a screen tries to do five things emotionally, simplify it.
2. **Reduce visual noise aggressively.** Premium apps hide complexity.
3. **Typography should carry emotion.** Fewer words. Bold confidence. Cinematic spacing.
4. **Make actions feel physical.** Haptics, spring physics, breathing motion, press states.
5. **The live capture screen is the emotional centerpiece.** The orb should feel reactive, ambient, intelligent, calm, trustworthy.
6. **Post-show is the most important moment.** Reflective, proud, rewarding, emotionally validating. Not administrative.
7. **Build around identity, not data.** Reinforce: performer, songwriter, working musician, creator.
8. **Native app standards.** Thumb-first, bottom-weighted actions, 44px minimum touch targets, no desktop layouts shrunk to mobile.
9. **Simplify before adding.** If something feels weak: reduce, clarify, focus.
10. **Setlistr should feel like part of the artist's team.** Tour support. Memory keeper. Quiet backstage companion.

## The voice

Copy should sound like it came from someone who has loaded into clubs at 1AM and wondered if any of it mattered.

**Good:** "11 songs. That's a show." / "That one's on record." / "The stage is quiet for now."

**Bad:** "Performance successfully recorded." / "Submit report to PRO." / "Data processed."

## What we are not building

- A dashboard
- An admin portal
- A music industry CRM
- Productivity software

## What we are building

A living archive of an artist's journey. The one thing that sees every show — small venue, off night, Tuesday in a bar with 12 people — and says: this happened, it counted, it's part of your story.
