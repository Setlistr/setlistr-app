# Access request: "not approved at this time" reply (manual template)

For an admin to send by hand from the team inbox when an access request isn't approved. Nothing in the app sends it, and there is no rejection status: the request stays in the Admin waitlist until someone removes it.

Before sending:
- Check the email address matches the request exactly. Approval and replies are tied to that address.
- Don't give a reason that judges the person's music or career.
- Don't promise a date or a later approval.
- Industry requests (publishers, labels, booking agents) are handled separately. Use a direct conversation, not this template.

---

**Subject:** Your Setlistr access request

Hi {first name},

Thanks for requesting access to Setlistr. We've reviewed your request, and we aren't able to approve an account at this time.

Setlistr access is approved individually while we work closely with a small number of artists and their teams. This isn't a judgement of your music.

If anything changes on your side, or you'd like us to take another look, reply to this email with your artist or project name and a link to your music.

Thanks again for your interest,
The Setlistr team

---

Notes for reviewers:
- The app is free to download, but an account opens only once it's approved. Don't describe it as "invite-only" or "beta".
- Setlistr helps artists prepare the details for filings they make with their PRO. It doesn't file claims, so don't imply that it does.
