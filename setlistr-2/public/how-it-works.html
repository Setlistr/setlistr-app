From Stage to Submission
01
Artist

Performs live. Opens Setlistr.

02
Mobile App

One-tap start. Show details auto-filled (GPS, planned setlist).

03
Audio Capture

App captures chunked audio throughout the performance.

04
Recognition (ACRCloud)

Chunks sent to ACRCloud for audio fingerprinting. Full candidate array returned for each chunk.

05
Recognition Logs

Every chunk, timestamp, all candidate results and scores are stored.

06

Reconciliation Engine

Our proprietary multi-signal Bayesian engine.

Cluster

Group raw observations into candidate song slots on the timeline

Resolve

Score & rank competing candidates using raw evidence + context + priors

Conclude

Assign confidence tier: Confirmed, Likely, or Unknown

07
Evidence Ledger

Every conclusion is traceable end-to-end. Who, what, when, why, how confident.

08
Verified Setlist (artist confirmation)

Engine presents results in tiers. Artist reviews and confirms any Likely songs with one tap.

CONFIRMED ≥ 0.95
LIKELY 0.60–0.95
UNKNOWN < 0.60
09
PRO Submission

Verified setlists formatted per PRO rules and submitted on the artist's behalf — SOCAN, ASCAP, BMI, PRS, APRA, SESAC, GMR.

10
Royalty Claim

PROs accept claims and attach them to the correct works & writers.

11
Career Record

Permanent, growing record of every show. Stats, maps, milestones, share cards, annual wrap.

12
Industry Intelligence

Aggregated, anonymized data powers insights for the entire live music ecosystem.

* Royalty figures shown are illustrative only. Actual royalties vary and are paid by PROs.
